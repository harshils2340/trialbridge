# BridgeMD cold outreach

For coordinators and site directors at small research sites.
Merge fields match `final_leads.csv`.

## The email

Subject: `one inbox for your applicants`

> Hey {{first_name}},
>
> I'm Malhar, I'm building BridgeMD. It puts all your applicants from ads,
> email and referrals into one inbox, and pre-screens them against your
> eligibility so you can see who actually qualifies.
>
> Flat monthly, nothing per patient.
>
> Worth 15 minutes? I can load one of your studies in so you're looking at
> your own criteria.
>
> Malhar
>
> [address] · [unsubscribe]

## Follow-ups

+4 days:

> Hey {{first_name}}, did this land? Happy to just send a short recording
> instead of booking a call.

+10 days:

> I'll leave it here. We're at bridgemd.health if it comes up later.

## Rules

- Flat licence only. Never price per referral, patient or enrollment
  (`COMPLIANCE.md` §4).
- Don't promise enrollment numbers.
- Don't imply we message their patients. The site sends everything.
- CAN-SPAM: address and opt-out on every send. Current leads are all US.
  Canadian leads need CASL consent first.
