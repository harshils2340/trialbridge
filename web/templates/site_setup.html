REDCap intake & screening

{{ ic.icon('activity') }} How to connect (takes about 10 minutes) {{ ic.icon('chevron-down') }}

REDCap is the tool you already use to keep track of patients. Follow these steps once and your patients will be able to fill out your form right here in BridgeMD. You do not need to be technical — if you get stuck, your REDCap administrator can help with any step.

Part 1 — In REDCap (the tool you already use)

  1. Open your study's project in REDCap — the same place you normally enter patient information.
  2. Turn your screening form into a survey. Open Online Designer, find your form, and click Enable as survey.
  3. Turn on the connection and copy your key. In the left menu click API, then click Generate token, then copy the long code it shows you.
  4. Copy your REDCap web address. It looks like https://redcap.yourhospital.org/api/ — copy the whole thing.

Part 2 — Here in BridgeMD

  1. Paste the web address into REDCap web address (API URL) and the long code into API token (a secure key) in the boxes below.
  2. Click Load my forms, then pick your screening form from the list in Your screening form.
  3. Check the box that says your form is approved and patients agree, then click Save REDCap connection.
