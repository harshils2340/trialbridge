{{ ic.icon('activity') }} Connect REDCap · about 10 minutes

REDCap is the tool you already use to keep track of patients. Do this once and your patients can fill out your form right here in BridgeMD. You don't need to be technical - if you get stuck, your REDCap administrator can help with any step.

Part 1 — In REDCap

  1. Open your study's project in REDCap - where you normally enter patient info.
  2. Turn your screening form into a survey: open Online Designer, find your form, click Enable as survey.
  3. In the left menu click API, then Generate token, and copy the long code.
  4. Copy your REDCap web address - it looks like https://redcap.yourhospital.org/api/.

Part 2 — Here in BridgeMD

  1. Paste the web address into REDCap web address and the code into API token.
  2. Click Load my forms, then choose your form under Your screening form.
  3. Tick the ethics-approval box, then click Save REDCap connection.
{{ ic.icon('check') }} Your token is like a password - kept hidden, never shared.
{{ ic.icon('check') }} Not sure? Your REDCap administrator can generate the token for you.
{{ ic.icon('check') }} Once connected, patients complete your form inside BridgeMD.
For your IT team (optional)

Prefer environment config for a single project? Set REDCAP_API_URL and REDCAP_API_TOKEN; per-site values above override it. Tokens are stored securely and never shown again. For completion callbacks, point a REDCap survey / Data-Entry-Trigger at {{ url_for('redcap_webhook', _external=True) }}.
