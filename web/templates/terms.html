What BridgeMD is

BridgeMD helps you find recruiting clinical trials (using public information from ClinicalTrials.gov) and apply to ones you're interested in. It is an information and matching tool.

What BridgeMD is not

  • It is not medical advice. Always talk to a qualified healthcare professional about your care.
  • It is not the study sponsor and does not run the trials.
  • It does not decide eligibility. Any "fit" signal is an estimate; the study team makes the final decision after their own screening.

No guarantee

Applying does not guarantee you'll be contacted, screened, or enrolled. Trial listings can be incomplete or out of date - we show the registry's information as-is and can't guarantee its accuracy. Verify important details with the study team.

Using the service

  • Provide accurate information about yourself, and only submit information you have the right to share.
  • Don't misuse the service (no scraping, automated abuse, or attempts to disrupt it).
  • You're responsible for keeping your account secure.
