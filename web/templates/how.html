From "I'm interested" to enrolled - clearly.

TrialBridge connects two sides: you (looking for a trial) and the study team (looking for the right participants). Here's exactly how an application moves between you, all the way through. Nothing happens without your consent.

{{ ic.icon('user-plus') }} Your side

  1. Find a trialSearch by condition and location. We read each study's eligibility in plain English.
  2. ApplyTap "I'm interested" and leave your contact. You consent to share it with that study's team.
  3. Connect your records (optional)Link your health record (like your hospital portal or Apple Health) so the team can pre-screen you from your real history - no re-typing.
  4. Get pre-screenedThe team checks whether you likely fit, using your details and any records you connected.
  5. Move to a screening visitIf you're likely eligible, you're invited to complete forms and in-person checks that confirm eligibility.
  6. EnrollOnce confirmed, you're enrolled - you were in control the whole way.
{{ ic.icon('activity') }}

{{ ic.icon('award') }} The study team's side

  1. Receive a consented applicationA real, interested person - not a cold lead - lands in their inbox.
  2. Pre-screen from recordsWith consent, they review the connected de-identified record instead of chasing paperwork.
  3. Confirm likely eligibilityThey mark the person "likely eligible" (or not a match) - and you see that update.
  4. Invite to the screening visitThey schedule the forms and in-person checks that officially confirm eligibility.
  5. EnrollConfirmed participants are enrolled and the loop is closed.
