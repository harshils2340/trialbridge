Find a trial. Apply in seconds. Stay in control.

TrialBridge helps you find clinical trials you may qualify for - without digging through medical jargon or filling endless forms. It's completely free, private, and nothing is shared without your say-so.

  1. SearchEnter your condition and location. We pull recruiting studies live from ClinicalTrials.gov and read each one's eligibility, so you see in plain English whether you may fit.
  2. Apply in secondsFound one that fits? Leave your name and how to reach you. The study team first reviews you de-identified - they only get your contact details if they accept you.
  3. Connect your records (optional)Link a health record so the team can pre-screen you from your real history instead of making you re-type everything.
  4. Get contactedIf you're likely eligible, the study team reaches out to set up a screening visit - and you can follow every step in My applications.
