  3. 3

    Open your inbox.

    Every lead lands in one place, with the facts pulled out and a reply already drafted.

    What happens: email, texts, Instagram DMs, web forms, voicemail and forwarded portal leads all arrive here, sorted into the views you named. Type a one-line instruction and {{ OMNI_BRAND }} writes the reply in your voice.
    What you decide: whether it sends. And anything you want changed later, you type: add a field for whether they have a lawyer, flag anyone mentioning a court date, auto reject anyone outside IL and IN.

What stays under your control

  • Sending. Nothing goes out without you unless you allow it. Anything {{ OMNI_BRAND }} sends on its own is labeled as sent by {{ OMNI_BRAND }}.
  • The facts. Every extracted field links to the message it came from, so you can check it in one click.
  • The setup. Fields, views, rules and tone all change by typing a sentence. You see the change before it applies.
  • Your data. No account. Your workspace lives in your browser, with a link you can share with your team.
