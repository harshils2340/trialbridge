What it does, and what stays yours

What it does

  • Pulls every lead into one place.Email, texts, Instagram DMs, web forms and voicemail. Portals that only send email (Zillow, Avvo, Zocdoc, Psychology Today, Indeed, Care.com) get forwarded to your {{ OMNI_BRAND }} address.
  • Reads each message and fills in fields.For a law firm: incident type, date, whether they already have a lawyer. You name the fields, it finds the answers.
  • Sorts leads into views you describe in plain words."Serious injuries with no lawyer." "Moving within two weeks." "Not a fit."
  • Drafts replies from a one-line instruction."Ask for the police report and offer Tuesday." Written in your voice, in the reply box.

What stays yours

  • Nothing sends without you.Every draft waits for a person to read and send it, unless you allow {{ OMNI_BRAND }} to handle something routine.
  • Anything it sends on its own is labeled.You can see exactly what went out and tighten the rules at any time.
  • Every fact shows where it came from.Click a field and see the message it was read from.
  • Change anything by typing."Auto reject anyone outside IL and IN." "Add a field for insurance carrier." No settings pages to learn.
