{# Objection-busting FAQ: answers the real fears people post before applying (scam? paid? safe? what happens next?). Truthful, neutral framing only - no sponsor recruitment claims, no inflated pay promises. Kept at the very end. #}
Your questions, answered

Questions people ask before applying

Is BridgeMD really free?
Yes, searching and applying are 100% free for patients. We're a matching tool, not the study sponsor, and we never charge you.
Will I get paid?
Many trials reimburse your time and travel, and some pay a stipend. The amount is set by each study, not by us. We show what the study lists. Never treat compensation as the only reason to join.
Is it safe? Am I just a guinea pig?
Every trial follows a protocol reviewed by an independent ethics board (IRB/REB). The study team walks you through the risks and what to expect before you agree to anything, and taking part is always voluntary. You can leave at any time.
What happens after I apply?
Your interest goes straight to that study's team. They contact you to confirm details and check whether you're eligible. Applying is not enrolling, so you're never obligated to continue.
Do I need a doctor's referral or insurance?
No referral is needed to apply. Trials don't bill your insurance for study procedures, and the study team will tell you exactly what's covered.
Will I get a placebo instead of real treatment?
Not all trials use a placebo. When one does, the study team explains it upfront, and participants are usually still monitored closely and continue receiving standard care.
Is my information private? Will I get spammed?
You control what you share. We don't sell your data. We only pass what you submit to the specific study team you choose to apply to.
