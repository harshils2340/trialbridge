- BridgeMD provides trial discovery, application routing, and workflow tools.
- BridgeMD is not the study sponsor and does not make final eligibility decisions.

## Accounts and acceptable use
- Customer is responsible for authorized user access and credential hygiene.
- No misuse, scraping abuse, or unlawful handling of patient data.

## Data and permissions
- Customer confirms it has legal authority to submit and process patient data.
- Customer controls sponsor/site decisions and communications with applicants.

## Service levels
- Best-effort service with planned maintenance windows.
- Incident and support response process documented separately.

## Privacy and security
- Technical/organizational safeguards are described in the Security Addendum.
- Business Associate/Addendum terms apply when PHI is processed.

## Liability / disclaimers
- Service is provided as contracted; trial outcomes are not guaranteed.
- Mutual limitations of liability and indemnity clauses to be finalized by counsel.
