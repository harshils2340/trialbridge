# BridgeMD Privacy Notice (template)

> Draft template only. Finalize with counsel before public publication.

## What we collect
- Account data (name, email, role).
- Application workflow data (trial, status, messages, scheduling metadata).
- Optional records integration outputs for pre-screen support.

## Why we process data
- Match applicants to studies.
- Route and manage intake for study teams.
- Improve enrollment/retention workflow quality and reporting.

## Sharing
- Data is shared with authorized study teams for claimed studies.
- Service providers process data under contract for hosting, email, and integrations.

## Retention and deletion
- Data retention periods should be defined per customer agreement/regulatory need.
- Deletion requests and legal hold processes should be documented operationally.

## Security
- Encryption in transit and at rest where supported by infrastructure.
- Role-based access and auditable workflow events.

## Contact
- Provide operational privacy contact email and escalation channel.
